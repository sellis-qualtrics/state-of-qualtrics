Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get my refund?

Most e-filed returns are processed within 14 days. Paper returns can take 8–10 weeks. Some returns are held for identity verification. Check your status.

I got a letter asking me to verify my identity. What do I do?

Letter TAX-ID-1 means we need to confirm you filed the return. Follow the instructions in the letter within 30 days. Your refund will be released after we verify your identity.

Is there a fee to pay by card?

Yes. Our card processor charges a 2.5% convenience fee. Bank account (ACH) payments are free.
