You'll need to renew in person

Licenses that expired more than 1 year ago can't be renewed online. You may need to retake the knowledge test. Book an appointment.

It's too early to renew

You can renew online starting 6 months before your license expires. We'll send you a reminder by mail.

A vision screening is required

Because of a vision or medical change, you'll need to renew in person. Book an appointment.
