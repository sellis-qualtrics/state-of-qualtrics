Vehicle registration

Register your vehicle within 30 days of buying it or moving to the State of Qualtrics. Renew your registration every year by the last day of the month on your sticker.

What you'll need

  • The vehicle title or a bill of sale
  • Proof of State of Qualtrics auto insurance
  • A safety and emissions inspection (for vehicles more than 4 years old)
  • Payment for registration fees

Renewing your registration?

You'll get a reminder by mail 45 days before it expires. Online renewal for registrations is coming in 2027. For now, renew at any service center.

Frequently asked questions

Can I renew my license online?

Yes, if your license expires within the next 6 months or expired less than 1 year ago, and you haven't had a vision or medical change. Start your renewal.

How long does it take to get my new license?

Standard mail takes 7–10 business days. Expedited delivery ($10) takes 3–5 business days. You can keep driving with your confirmation email until it arrives.

What do I need for a road test?

Bring your learner permit, a registered and insured vehicle, and a licensed driver age 21 or older. Book a road test.

Where can I get the driver handbook?

The 2026 State of Qualtrics Driver Handbook covers traffic laws, road signs, and safe driving. Printed copies are free at every service center.
