How to apply

  1. Check if you qualify

    Answer a few questions to see which programs may fit your household. About 5 minutes.

    Start the screener

  2. Apply online

    One application covers SNAP, Medicaid, cash assistance, and child care help. About 20 minutes.

    Start an application

  3. Complete your interview

    A caseworker will call you within 10 business days. Most decisions are made within 30 days.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for more than one program at once?

Yes. One online application covers SNAP, Medicaid, cash assistance, and child care assistance.

What documents will I need?

Proof of identity, proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs or an employer letter), and proof of address. You can upload them, mail them, or bring them to an office.

How long does it take to get a decision?

Most applications are decided within 30 days. If you have very little income, you may get SNAP within 7 days.
