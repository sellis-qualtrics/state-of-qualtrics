How unemployment benefits work

  1. File your claim

    Apply online in about 25 minutes. Have your employer information ready.

  2. Serve your waiting week

    The first eligible week is unpaid. You still need to certify for it.

  3. Certify every week

    Each week, report any work and at least 3 job search contacts.

  4. Get paid

    Payments arrive by direct deposit or debit card 2–3 days after you certify.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get benefits if I quit my job?

Possibly. If you quit for a good reason connected to the job, you may still qualify. We'll review your situation after you file.

What counts as a job search contact?

Applying for a job, interviewing, attending a job fair, or meeting with a Workforce Center counselor. You need at least 3 each week.

Why did my session time out?

For your security, claim forms sign you out after a few minutes without activity. Have your information ready before you start.
