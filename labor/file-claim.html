Your claim will need a review

Because your job ended this way, a claims specialist will review your situation. You can still file today. We'll ask you to explain what happened.

You can't file while working full time

Unemployment benefits are for people who lost their job or had their hours cut. If your hours are reduced later, you can file then.

File in the state where you worked

You need to file with the state where you worked in the last 18 months. Call 1-555-562-7000 if you worked in more than one state.
